Apple's APAC

  • Apple Positional Audio Codec. In the OS since iOS 13, formally defined with iOS 18.
  • Carries channel-based and scene-based (Ambisonics) audio.
  • Delivery format for Apple Spatial Audio Format (ASAF), shown at WWDC25.
  • Required for Apple Immersive Video. Reported up to 768 kbps.
  • Streams over HLS, with loudness and DRC metadata embedded.

For game audio

  • Useful for linear content: cinematics, trailers, immersive video.
  • Apple platforms only, encoded with Apple's OS tools.
  • A delivery codec. Your engine still renders the interactive mix.
