A decision order

Ask in this order. Stop at the first yes.

1

Short, frequent, latency-critical?

ADPCM, or PCM if memory allows

2

Dialogue or voice chat?

Opus, at speech bitrates

3

Long and streamed from disk?

Opus or Vorbis

4

CPU-bound at peak voice count?

Platform codec: XMA, ATRAC9, AAC

Then listen on target hardware, at real mix levels, before locking bitrates.
