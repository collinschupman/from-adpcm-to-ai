The basics

23

Hardware acceleration is not a general speed-up: each platform has dedicated hardware for one specific codec, and only that codec benefits. Xbox has decoded XMA in hardware since the Xbox 360, which handled up to 320 XMA voices; Xbox One's SHAPE audio block raised that to 512. Xbox Series X|S moved its hardware decoder to Opus through the new audio processor, and keeps SHAPE for older content. Nintendo Switch also has a hardware Opus decoder, running libopus on its audio DSP. It is a small pool: one Wwise user hit 'Failed creating Opus decoder' errors and fixed them by keeping Opus for music and large files and using ADPCM for everything else. PlayStation uses ATRAC9, Sony's codec optimized for games, through its SDK; the specifics of what is hardware-decoded on PS4 and PS5 are in Sony's developer documentation, which is under NDA. Most phones have hardware AAC decoders. Everything else, Vorbis, Bink Audio and RAD Audio included, decodes on the CPU. And two catches: hardware decoders are a fixed resource, so plan which sounds get them; and in Unreal, the Platform Specific compression setting does not currently support seeking.
