[Slide: the dashed dropdown is a placeholder for a real middleware screenshot, such as Wwise conversion settings or Unreal's compression type.] In my experience, audio technology in games is underappreciated for how deep it goes, and codecs are a great example. I've worked on teams where even experienced sound designers weren't sure what they were really choosing when they picked a codec in the engine, because it's usually just a dropdown. Having gone deep on things like bringing up new codecs, I figured I could help demystify them for teams trying to ship high-quality audio. So, how are we going to do that?
