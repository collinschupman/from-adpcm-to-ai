Hardware

Several codecs exist because of a specific hardware limit.

Dedicated decoders

Sound chips and DSPs decode ADPCM, XMA or AAC, so the CPU doesn't have to.

Battery

Hardware decode draws less power, which matters on handhelds and phones.

Storage media

Optical discs seek slowly, so music and VO stream as compressed data. Cartridge and download sizes cap the total.

Output links

Optical S/PDIF carries only stereo PCM, and Bluetooth is narrower still. Both need a codec on the way out.
