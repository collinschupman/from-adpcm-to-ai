Why this talk

I've found codec tech can be intimidating, especially on teams without a large audio programming group. Yet it has a real impact on the project.

This talk aims to build that understanding by going over:

How they work

Raw PCM audio, and what a codec does to shrink it

The problems

What kinds of problems codecs solve: memory, disk, voice chat, streaming and hardware limits

The history

How they came up, from ADPCM to Opus

Today

How they're used now: Opus, Vorbis, AAC and the platform codecs

The near future

How they might be used next: APAC, QOA and neural codecs

You leave with a better understanding of codecs, and a way to apply it to your own project.
