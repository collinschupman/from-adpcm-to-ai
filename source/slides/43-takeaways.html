Takeaways

1

This is an optimization. At the start of a project, your engine's defaults are fine; revisit codecs once you know your budgets.

2

Codec choice is a budget decision: CPU, memory, disk and quality at once.

3

Decide per asset class. ADPCM for short SFX, Opus for VO and streams, platform codecs where hardware helps, and Bink Audio as the starting point on Unreal.

4

On the frontier: QOA for sound effects, APAC and IAMF for spatial delivery, and neural tools reaching voice chat first.
