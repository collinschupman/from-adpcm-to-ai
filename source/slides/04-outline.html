Introduction

4

I've divided the talk into four sections. We won't go too deep on the technical side, and I won't be showing any code. First, the basics: what audio data is, the tradeoffs every codec setting makes between size, quality and CPU, the main families of codec, how the two that games rely on work, and the codecs teams actually ship today. Then, how to choose: a decision order you can take back to your project, one rule worth remembering, and what to measure. Finally, we'll peek at the frontier: what codec tech is on the horizon, and what game teams and game tech companies should keep an eye on. We'll close with takeaways and some final thoughts. But first, a quick story that we'll tie everything back to at the end.
