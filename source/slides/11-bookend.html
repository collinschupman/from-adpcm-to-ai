~45 sec. Fast forward five years. Same studio, Respawn, same problem: lots of sounds and a CPU budget. In 2014 Titanfall's answer was no codec at all, 35 GB of raw PCM. In 2019 Apex Legends, by Epic's account, uses Bink Audio for all playback with 80 to 100 voices at once. The problem did not go away; the codec options got better, and the team chose differently. That journey is what the rest of this talk unpacks: what those choices are, what each one costs, and how to pick on purpose. Say the link between the two games as your own observation; neither Epic nor Respawn frames it that way.
