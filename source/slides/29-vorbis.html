The basics

29

Vorbis is the codec that made perceptual compression normal in games. Xiph.org released 1.0 in 2002, it is royalty-free, and it became the long-time default in Wwise and many engines. Unreal's current sound compression options are Bink Audio, RAD Audio, ADPCM, PCM, Opus, platform-specific and project-defined, so Vorbis is no longer offered there. On decode cost, Audiokinetic's measurements put Vorbis at 2,300 to 5,700 streams on a 2018 Core i7 against about 9,500 for ADPCM and 500 to 1,300 for Opus: cheaper than Opus, far costlier than ADPCM. It sounds very good at mid to high bitrates and falls behind Opus at low ones, so it suits music, ambience and long streams. Why not MP3: MP3's frame-based encoder adds delay and padding, so loops click or gap unless your tools trim it, and it needed a patent licence until the patents ran out around 2017. Vorbis did the same job for free and loops cleanly.
