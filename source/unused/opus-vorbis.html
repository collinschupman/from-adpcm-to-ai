Vorbis and Opus

The incumbent

Vorbis

  • Royalty-free; 1.0 released in 2002
  • Long-time default in Wwise and many engines
  • Mature encoders, predictable decode
  • Falls behind at low bitrates

The challenger

Opus

  • IETF standard, RFC 6716 (2012)
  • SILK for speech, CELT for music
  • 6 to 510 kbps; frames from 2.5 to 60 ms
  • Best quality per bit at low rates

Default new streamed content to Opus. Keep Vorbis where a pipeline already depends on it.

~2.5 min. Opus was built for real-time comms, which is why it's so strong on speech and at low bitrates; CELT handles music, SILK handles speech, and hybrid mode blends them. Vorbis is still perfectly good at higher bitrates and is everywhere. The honest framing: Opus is the better default for new work, but switching a shipped pipeline isn't free.
